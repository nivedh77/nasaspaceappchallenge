# 30-second demonstration plan

**0–4 seconds:** Show STRANGER's title and explain: “STRANGER investigates long-term environmental signals using NASA POWER data.”

**4–9 seconds:** Select Kochi, Kerala and the temperature variable. Keep the default 2001–2025 period.

**9–16 seconds:** Click “Run Investigation”. Wait for the NASA response and show the annual time-series chart.

**16–23 seconds:** Point to Theil–Sen slope, Kendall tau and p-value. Say whether the result is or is not statistically significant, using the result actually shown by the app.

**23–28 seconds:** Show the source citation and the methodological caveat. Briefly mention that this is a point location, not a state-wide average.

**28–30 seconds:** Show the CSV/report export buttons and finish.

Record only after the deployed app has been tested. Keep the finished video at or below 30 seconds.
